How to make a debt payoff plan in 4 steps

A payoff plan doesn't need a spreadsheet or an app. It needs your real numbers, one method, and a way to see your progress.

1. List every debt

Write down each balance, interest rate (APR) and minimum payment. If you're not sure what you owe, your credit reports are free at annualcreditreport.com.

2. Pick one method

Snowball targets the smallest balance first. Avalanche targets the highest rate first. See how they compare.

3. Find extra money each month

Every extra dollar goes to your target debt. Common places to find it: unused subscriptions, a cheaper phone plan, meal planning, and selling things you don't use. You can also call your card company and ask for a lower interest rate.

4. Track every payment

Log each payment and watch the balance fall. When one debt is gone, roll its payment into the next one. Recheck your debt-free date every three months.

Watch out for

  • Debt-relief companies that ask for fees before they do anything.
  • Payday or title loans to cover payments.
  • New charges on cards you're paying down.

For free help, look for a nonprofit credit counselor (nfcc.org).
